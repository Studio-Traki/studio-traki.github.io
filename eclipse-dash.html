Eclipse Dash

One tap to flip. It starts slow, then it gets fast.

Free to play on iPhone. Contains ads and optional in-app purchases.

  • Tap to flip between the inside and outside of the ring.
  • Graze obstacles at the last moment for bonus points and build up to an x5 combo.
  • Trigger the Eclipse, where every point counts double.
  • A new Daily Run every day. Short runs, one-tap retry.
