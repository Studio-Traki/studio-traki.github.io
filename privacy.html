Games Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Short version: Traki games have no accounts, and we run no servers. Your game progress stays on your phone. Our games show ads through Google AdMob and sell optional items through the App Store (and later Google Play), with RevenueCat checking purchases. Those companies collect some data, described below. You can say no to ad tracking, change your ad choices at any time, and ask us questions or ask us to delete data.

Who we are

Traki (“Traki”, “we”, “us”) is the name under which Vaja Chelidze, Tbilisi, Georgia (the country), publishes mobile games. Vaja Chelidze is responsible for your data under this policy (the “controller” under EU and UK law). Contact: notvaja@gmail.com.

This policy covers our mobile games and this website.

What we collect ourselves

  • Nothing from inside the game. Our games don’t ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. They have no sign-in, and the game itself doesn’t send anything to us.
  • Game progress (scores, shards, skins, streak, settings) is saved only on your device. We can’t see it. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact support, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you.
  • Reports from our partners. AdMob and RevenueCat give us statistics, such as how many ads were shown, revenue, and purchase counts by country. We don’t use them to identify you.

What our partners collect

Our games include software (SDKs) from the companies below. They collect data when the game runs, and they use it as described here and in their own policies.

Google AdMob and Google’s consent tool (ads)

AdMob shows the ads in our games: optional reward ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Google’s consent tool asks for your ad choices where the law requires it. To show, measure and, if you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android) and other app or device identifiers
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and taps
  • information about the ads you have seen
  • technical data, such as device model, OS version, crash logs and performance data

On iPhone, Google gets the advertising ID only if you allow tracking. If you don’t, you still see ads, but they are less personalised. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

RevenueCat (purchase checks)

RevenueCat confirms your purchases so they work and can be restored. It receives a random ID created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple ID), your purchase and transaction records, and basic device data (OS, app version, country and IP address). Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple and Google (stores and payments)

Apple (and Google, once our games are on Google Play) handles downloads and payments under its own policy. We never see your payment details. See Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks before allowing tracking. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you see a consent message before any personalised ads. Change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Fewer ads: buying “Remove Ads” turns off the ads between runs. Reward ads stay available, but only if you choose to watch them.
  • Report an ad: if you see an ad that is offensive or not suitable, tell us. See Report an ad.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal data for targeted advertising. We don’t sell personal data for money. Personalised ads through AdMob may count as “sharing” under some US state laws. You can opt out by turning tracking off (iPhone), deleting your Advertising ID (Android), or using Privacy options in the game.

Because we don’t hold data about you ourselves, the controls above are usually the fastest route. You can also email us. We’ll answer within one month and help with requests to Google or RevenueCat. For purchase records, we may ask for the order ID from your Apple receipt so we can find them.

In the EU, EEA or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience aged 13 and older and are not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used one of our games and data was collected, contact us and we’ll help get it removed.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat are based in the United States and may process data there and in other countries. When data from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland goes abroad, they rely on approved safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as described in their own policies.

How long data is kept

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game or reset progress. Support emails are kept for up to two years after the conversation ends, then deleted. Google and RevenueCat keep data for the periods set out in their own policies. All communication between our games and these services is encrypted in transit.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers of its own. GitHub may log your IP address for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.
