Support

Something not working? We read every email.

Email notvaja@gmail.com

Include the game name, your device model and OS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Eclipse Dash

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flip your sun between the outside and the inside of the ring. Avoid the obstacles, and pass close to them (a “graze”) to build combos. Chain enough combos to trigger an Eclipse. You can replay the tutorial from Settings → Show tutorial again.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Remove Ads turns off ads between runs. Rewarded ads, the ones you choose to watch for a revive or bonus shards, stay available as an option.

I got a new phone. How do I get my purchases back?

Install the game, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, and tap Settings → Restore purchases.

Can I keep my scores and skins on a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the game or switching phones starts fresh. Purchases are always restorable.

I saw an ad that is offensive or not suitable. How do I report it?

In the game, open Settings → Report an ad, or email notvaja@gmail.com with the subject “Ad report”. Tell us what the ad showed, the advertiser or app it promoted, and roughly when you saw it. A screenshot helps. We review every report and block ads that break our rules.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy options in the game. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play’s refund help.

How do I see or delete my data?

Eclipse Dash sends no game data to us; your progress lives on your phone. Ad and purchase data is held by Google and RevenueCat. Email us and we’ll help with access or deletion requests. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the game have flashing effects?

Yes. Some moments use bright flashes and fast-moving light. Turn on Settings → Reduce motion to soften flashes and remove screen shake. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, check with a doctor before playing.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset progress. It asks twice, because it can’t be undone. Purchases are kept.

Anatomy Blitz

I found a mistake in a question

Thank you. Tap “Report a problem with this question” under the explanation, pick a reason and, if you like, add a short note (please don’t include personal information). Or use Settings → Help & legal → Report a content error, or email notvaja@gmail.com with the question text. We check every report and fix confirmed errors in the next content update.

Is Anatomy Blitz medical advice?

No. Anatomy Blitz is a study aid for anatomy. It doesn’t replace your course materials, teachers or a qualified health professional. Anatomy references sometimes differ on details; for exams, follow the sources your course uses.

Someone has an offensive name. How do I report or hide them?

On any leaderboard, league table or challenge result, tap ⋯ next to the name and choose “Report name” or “Hide this player”. Hidden players disappear from your lists on this device. We aim to review reports within 48 hours and can reset names or ban players. You can also email notvaja@gmail.com.

Why is my name on a leaderboard?

When you earn XP or play the Daily Challenge, your display name and score appear on the leaderboards and in your weekly league. The default name looks like “Player#1234”. Change it any time in Profile. We don’t show or record your country.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Delete my online data. This deletes your player profile, display name, XP, scores, league history, challenges and the reports you sent from our server straight away; backup copies are gone within 14 days. Your study progress on the phone stays until you reset it (Settings → Reset progress) or delete the app. If you turn online features back on afterwards (Settings → Privacy), the game creates a new, separate profile; until then it stays offline.

Lost your phone, or deleted the app first? Email notvaja@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. Include your player ID (Settings → Privacy → Player ID) if you have it, or your display name and roughly when you last played. We’ll confirm when it’s done, within one month at most. Deleting data doesn’t cancel a subscription: cancel that in your Apple ID settings.

How do I cancel Anatomy Blitz Pro or the free trial?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Anatomy Blitz → Cancel. You can also open it from the game: Settings → Purchases → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel the subscription.

I bought Pro but it’s locked on my new phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with, then tap Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play’s refund help.

Can I keep my progress and league history on a new phone?

Not yet. Study progress is saved on your device, and your online profile is tied to it. A new phone starts a new profile. Purchases are always restorable.

Does Anatomy Blitz work offline?

Yes. Quizzes, Weak Spots reviews and the diagrams work offline. The Daily Challenge, leaderboards, leagues and friend challenges need a connection. XP you earn offline is sent when you’re back online.

How do I change my ad privacy choices or report an ad?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy → Privacy options (ads). On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To report an ad, use Settings → Help & legal → Report an ad, or see Report an ad above. Anatomy Blitz Pro has no ads.
