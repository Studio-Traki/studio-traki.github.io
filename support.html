Eclipse Dash

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flip your sun between the outside and the inside of the ring. Avoid the obstacles, and pass close to them (a “graze”) to build combos. Chain enough combos to trigger an Eclipse. You can replay the tutorial from Settings → Show tutorial again.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Remove Ads turns off ads between runs. Rewarded ads, the ones you choose to watch for a revive or bonus shards, stay available as an option.

I got a new phone. How do I get my purchases back?

Install the game, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, and tap Settings → Restore purchases.

Can I keep my scores and skins on a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the game or switching phones starts fresh. Purchases are always restorable.

I saw an ad that is offensive or not suitable. How do I report it?

In the game, open Settings → Report an ad, or email notvaja@gmail.com with the subject “Ad report”. Tell us what the ad showed, the advertiser or app it promoted, and roughly when you saw it. A screenshot helps. We review every report and block ads that break our rules.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy options in the game. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play’s refund help.

How do I see or delete my data?

We don’t keep game data about you; your progress lives on your phone. Ad and purchase data is held by Google and RevenueCat. Email us and we’ll help with access or deletion requests. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the game have flashing effects?

Yes. Some moments use bright flashes and fast-moving light. Turn on Settings → Reduce motion to soften flashes and remove screen shake. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, check with a doctor before playing.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset progress. It asks twice, because it can’t be undone. Purchases are kept.
